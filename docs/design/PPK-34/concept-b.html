Concept B · Card Club

A bold, tactile take on the card game. Everyone sits around a green felt poker table. The active ticket is dealt into the middle and your vote cards fan out like a hand of playing cards, with the one you pick raised. Tickets are a stack of index cards in a side rail, and the host controls sit in a "dealer tray" with a stork dealer in a visor and bow tie. Revealed cards flip face up at each seat, and the results look like stacks of chips.

The animals are flat sticker-style illustrations with thick outlines, like enamel pins. The type uses a slab serif, and the colours are cream card stock with brass and tomato red.
