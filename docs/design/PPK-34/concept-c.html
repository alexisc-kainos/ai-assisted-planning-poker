Concept C · Night Safari

A sleek dark-mode dashboard: a night sky, near-opaque glass panels and mint and amber neon accents for focus, selection and the primary action. On desktop it works like a control room, with a ticket list on the left, a central stage for the active ticket and vote tiles, and a roster on the right. In the roster, each person's ring shows whether they've voted, are still choosing or are spectating, with a text label as well. On mobile, jump links take you to Vote, Tickets or People.

The animals are glowing line art with amber eyes. The mascot scene is a capybara and a fox stargazing on a hill under a constellation shaped like a stork.
