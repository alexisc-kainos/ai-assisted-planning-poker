Planning Poker · animal-themed UI concepts

Design spike for PPK-34. These are three different visual directions to choose from. Each one has static mockups of the session lobby, voting and revealed results screens at desktop (1440 px) and mobile (390 px) widths. The screens use the app's real content and behaviour. They're for exploration only and don't change the app.

  • A · Riverside Lounge

    A calm, editorial look with soft neutrals and a serif type. A capybara relaxes in a hot spring.

    View concept A

  • B · Card Club

    Feels like a real card game: a felt table with seats around it, a hand of cards, and a stork as the dealer.

    View concept B

  • C · Night Safari

    A dark-mode dashboard with glassy panels and neon accents. The animals are drawn like constellations.

    View concept C

Product owner choice: A · Riverside Lounge. A follow-up ticket will cover applying it to the app.
