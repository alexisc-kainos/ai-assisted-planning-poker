Concept A · Riverside Lounge

A calm, editorial direction with warm sand neutrals, muted sage and teal, serif headings, lots of whitespace and thin dividers. The room lives in a quiet left sidebar with the share link and who's present. The wide main column is kept for the ticket, the vote and the results. Vote cards are soft, pebble-like shapes.

The animals are gentle flat illustrations with thin outlines. The mascot is a capybara with a yuzu on its head, relaxing in a hot spring while a stork watches from the reeds.

1. Session lobby

Priya (host) has just created the room. All four people have joined, three tickets have been added and none is active yet.

2. Voting

Ticket 1 is active. Priya has picked 5, Tom has voted, Aisha is still choosing and Ben is spectating. Nobody can see vote values yet.

3. Revealed results

Votes have been revealed: Priya 5, Tom 5, Aisha 8. Ben is spectating, so he isn't included. 5 is the most common vote.
